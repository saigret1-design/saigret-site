The offer

Symbolic cover art for singles and EPs.

This is for musicians who know what their release feels like, but do not yet have the image. I take the song, lyrics, title, or emotional direction and reduce it into a cover built around one clear visual idea.

Best for

Darkwave, metal, industrial, post-punk, ambient, experimental, emo, noise, horror synth, and other emotionally heavy music.

You provide

The track, lyrics, title, mood, references, or a rough explanation of what the release means. It does not need to be perfectly visual yet.

You receive

A square cover image built around the emotional core of the release, ready for digital platforms and promotion.

Process

A simple process for turning sound into image.

01

Send the release

Send the song, lyrics, title, mood, references, or the emotion behind the release. A rough explanation is enough.

02

I find the visual center

I translate the theme into a symbol, mood, and composition so the cover has a clear emotional hook.

03

You get the cover

You receive the final square cover for your release. Promo crops can be added if agreed before the project starts.
