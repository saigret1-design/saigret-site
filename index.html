Levels of work

Choose the level of symbolic depth your project needs.

Some ideas need a sharp mark. Others need a developed visual metaphor. Some need a full symbolic illustration with stronger emotional and narrative weight.

Level 1

Symbolic Mark

A focused visual symbol built around one core idea. Best for personal marks, project emblems, identity seeds, or simple symbolic concepts.

Level 2

Symbolic Concept Piece

A stronger visual metaphor with mood, composition, and emotional direction. Best when your idea needs more than a simple mark.

Level 3

Full Symbolic Illustration

A complete symbolic composition for covers, campaigns, story visuals, or concepts that need deeper narrative impact.

Process

A simple process so you do not have to micromanage the art.

01

02

03

You get a clear image
